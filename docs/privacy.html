Privacy policy

Banyan collects nothing about you, your organisation or your payments.

Applies to the Banyan app for Mac, from the Mac App Store and as a direct download. Last updated 21 November 2026.

What stays on your Mac

The register (your entities, cheques, cheque books and notes), your printer calibration and the PDFs Banyan draws are kept in one folder on your Mac. They are never sent to the developer. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The optional Banyan server

If your organisation runs its own Banyan server and you connect to it with a key, Banyan sends the register to that server after each change, and members can read reports from it. That server belongs to your organisation, at an address it gives you; the developer does not run it and cannot see it. Without a server, nothing leaves your Mac.

Your network and your printer

Banyan talks to your printer through the Mac’s own print system, and may check on your local network that the printer is switched on. Downloads of new versions of the direct-download app come from the project’s release page on GitHub, which sees an ordinary web request.

Children

Banyan is a tool for organisations’ accounts and is not directed at children.

Questions

Ask on the project’s GitHub issues. If this policy ever changes, the change will be on this page and in the source history.
