Banyan Desk runs the money side of an organization from one Mac. Know what is due, pay it properly, keep a record nobody can quietly change, and hand your committee reports they can read.

Made for organizations run by small teams and volunteers: societies, associations, clubs, trusts, schools and small businesses.

KNOW WHAT IS DUE
• Everyone you pay in one place: staff, vendors, utilities, contracts and taxes, each with a category and notes.
• Banyan learns how often each payment recurs and shows what is due and what is overdue.

PAY PROPERLY
• Print cheques straight onto your bank's leaves: date, payee, amount in words and figures, and the crossing, each in its measured place.
• Batches print one leaf first, so a misfeed costs a leaf, not a book.
• Paid by hand? Record it in the same register.

A RECORD YOU CAN TRUST
• Every payment, by book and leaf. Nothing is deleted; a mistake is voided and the reason kept.
• Find anything by payee, number, amount or note.

REPORTS FOR THE COMMITTEE
• The year by category, quarter and payee, each period set against the last.
• Monthly reports as PDF, and CSV exports of everything.
• Open any figure to see the payments behind it, and fix a category or note in place.

SHARE, IF YOU WANT TO
Connect your organization's own Banyan server and members can read the reports and help keep categories right, without ever touching the register.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your records are one file on your Mac.

Cheques follow India's CTS-2010 standard, with amounts in lakh and crore and the year from April to March. Calibrate any printer with one test print on plain paper.
