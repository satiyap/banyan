Thank you for reviewing Banyan.

No account or sign-in is needed. On first launch Banyan offers to connect to a Banyan server; choose "Skip for now". The app is complete without a server: the server is optional, run by the organisation itself, and only adds register sync and read-only reports for its members. Its address is given privately to each organisation, so there is no demo server.

To try the app without a printer:
1. Desk: choose "New entity", give it a name and the payee printed on the cheque, and save.
2. Tray: write a cheque to it with an amount. The leaf preview above the rows shows exactly where each field will print.
3. Choose "Record as handwritten", then "Record it". The cheque goes into the register as if it had been written by hand; printing is the only step that needs a printer.
4. Open Register to see the leaf, and Year and Monthly to see it in the reports. In Year, open the entity to see its cheques; any cheque opens a panel to change its category or note.

Banyan prints only onto genuine bank-issued cheque leaves loaded in the user's printer. It never draws a cheque, security artwork, a signature or a MICR line on blank paper. It uses the printing entitlement to send the composed page to the macOS print system at 100% scale, the network client entitlement for the optional server and to check the printer is switched on, and the Downloads entitlement to save CSV and PDF exports there.

The cheque preview is drawn over a photograph of the kind of leaf the app was measured for (a State Bank of India multi-city cheque), with the account, name and MICR line removed, so a treasurer can see where each field lands on their own bank's leaf. Banyan has no affiliation with the bank and does not use its name or marks for anything else.
